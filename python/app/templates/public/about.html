About the project

An academic lab for transparent aviation decisions

AeroRoute AI demonstrates how competing operational objectives can be modeled, optimized and explained — without hiding the mechanics behind a black box.

Academic safety boundary

AeroRoute AI is strictly an academic decision-support prototype and is not certified under FAA Part 121 / 135 or ICAO standards. Real-world operations additionally require convective SIGMETs, TAF forecasts, PIREPs and sector-level airspace constraints. Use this project for study, benchmarking and demonstration only.
